### Tier 2, Phase 1 — listwise reranker on real-LoCoMo (RRF vs reranked)
Run date: 2026-06-08. Benchmark: locomo_real (conv-26), 12 multi-hop QA, shared-graph.
Reranker + reader + judge: meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-Turbo (Together), temp=0.
Method: each variant ingests the SAME conversation into its OWN clean graph (Neo4j wiped
between), so only the search/rerank differs. Then scored two ways: extractive token_recall
AND the SOTA-axis J-score (an LLM composes an answer from the retrieved facts; a second LLM
grades it vs the reference — the metric Mem0/Zep report).

## Result
========================================================================
variant                      n  token_recall   J-acc  correct  judge_tok
------------------------------------------------------------------------
RRF (baseline)              12        0.1944  0.0833     1/12      16040
Cross-Encoder rerank        12        0.1250  0.1667     2/12      15337
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Deltas (Cross-Encoder vs RRF):
  token_recall: -0.0694   (DOWN — expected, see below)
  J-score:      +0.0834   (0.0833 -> 0.1667; 1/12 -> 2/12 correct)

## The two metrics move in OPPOSITE directions — and that is the whole point
The reranker LOWERED token_recall but RAISED J-score. This is not a contradiction; it is the
q011 lesson at scale. token_recall rewards lexical overlap with the reference, so a vague,
word-sharing distractor inflates it without answering the question. The reranker demotes those
distractors out of the top-k — which costs token_recall — and promotes the facts that actually
answer the question — which the generative reader then composes correctly. We are optimizing
the axis SOTA reports (J-score), and on that axis reranking helped.

## The clean win (q004 — a real retrieval improvement, not judge noise)
q004 "What is Caroline's identity?"  reference = "Transgender"
  - RRF top facts: "Embracing Identity represents Caroline's journey of acceptance",
    "Caroline created Embracing Identity", ... (heavy "identity" word-overlap, but vague).
    Reader could only infer "LGBTQ" -> judged WRONG.
  - Cross-Encoder top fact (reranked to #1): "Caroline is attending a transgender conference."
    Reader answered "Transgender." -> judged CORRECT.
  The reranker pulled the one specifically-relevant fact above the vague lexical-overlap
  facts. This is the listwise reranker (reranker.py) doing exactly what it was built to do.

## Why this needed a new reranker at all
Graphiti's stock OpenAIRerankerClient ranks by reading per-token logprobs of a forced
"True"/"False", nudged with logit_bias={'6432','7983'} (OpenAI tokenizer IDs). Together/Llama
returns an EMPTY logprobs.content, so the stock client's zip(passages, scores, strict=True)
raises — the cross_encoder recipe crashes on every non-OpenAI backend (verified). We replaced
it with a backend-agnostic ListwiseReranker: ONE strict-structured-output call scores all
passages at once (RankGPT-style), Pydantic-validated, usage-counted, fail-closed to input
order. Portable to local models — the TYPHON thesis (scaffolding > model size).

## Honest reading
- n=1 conversation, 12 QA. The J gain is +1 correct answer (q004). A signal, not a sweep;
  variance at this scale is one flipped question = +8.3%. Report it as such.
- Both variants re-ingest (temp=0, so near-identical graphs, not bit-identical). A stricter
  design ingests once and searches the one graph two ways — a Phase-1.5 cleanup.
- Both variants hit the 16K extraction cap on a couple of episodes (Tier 3); equal to both.
- We remain far below SOTA (75-92%); reranking is one lever. Still open: PPR multi-hop,
  query decomposition (rest of Tier 2), and the extraction cap (Tier 3).
