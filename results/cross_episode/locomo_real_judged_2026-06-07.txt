Run date: 2026-06-07. Benchmark: locomo_real (conv-26), 12 multi-hop QA, shared-graph.
Reader + judge: meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-Turbo (Together), temp=0.
Method: post-hoc over the SAME retrieved facts each baseline saw (typhon.eval.generation +

This is the metric every agent-memory SOTA number uses (Mem0/Zep/...), unlike extractive recall.

## Why this matters more than token_recall (the q011 smoking gun)
q011 "Where did Caroline move from 4 years ago?"  reference = "Sweden"
  - extractive token_recall = 1.0 (PERFECT) — because a retrieved fact contained the word
    "Sweden": "Caroline received a necklace from her grandma in Sweden".
  - that fact does NOT answer the question. The generative reader said "I don't know"; the judge
    scored it WRONG. => token_recall was a FALSE POSITIVE; the J-score is honest.

## Honest reading
- The reader is conservative — it says "I don't know" when the retrieved facts don't contain the
  answer (10/12 for attention, several for graphiti). It does NOT hallucinate. Good behavior; the
  low score reflects genuine retrieval gaps, not a broken reader.
- attention 0/12: structurally out-of-window, so it never holds the cross-session fact (10 "I
  don't know" + 2 wrong guesses). Expected.
- graphiti 2/12: recalls cross-session facts (q002 fields, q004 identity) but the right fact is
  often missing from the graph or out-ranked. Two extraction calls hit the 16K cap (lost facts).
- The judge has known leniency (q002 "Art, social work, counseling" graded correct vs ref
  "Psychology, counseling certification" — shares "counseling"). LLM-judge noise; report as such.
- n=1 conversation, 12 QA — a signal, not a sweep.
