## Honest caveats
- Absolute recall is low for BOTH baselines: the shared prediction path is extractive
  (build_prediction_block selects retrieved text), not a generative LLM composing answers.
  The comparison isolates RETRIEVAL quality; the +66% relative is the signal, not the floor.
- 2 extraction calls hit the 16K completion-length cap and failed even after one retry
  (Llama-3.3-70B over-generates on the messier real episodes). Some facts were lost, and
  the ~33K wasted completion tokens are included in the $0.46. Better/chunked extraction
  would likely widen the lift — consistent with the standing "extraction is the bottleneck".
- n=1 conversation, 12 QA: a generalization SIGNAL beyond the synthetic probes, not a full
  LoCoMo sweep. Per-sample graphs + synthetic valid-times remain (baseline limitations).
